## Brand & Style

This design system expresses high-precision automotive engineering, algorithmic transparency, and institutional reliability tailored for modern vehicle commerce and technical telematics. It translates the speed, technical clarity, and precision of aerospace and automotive interfaces into a refined, high-density consumer super-app.

The visual style merges technical neo-grotesque discipline with utilitarian clarity:
- **Graphite & Titanium Tone**: Grounded in heavy slate values that eliminate frivolous ornamentation in favor of data integrity and structural legibility.
- **Instrument-Cluster Precision**: Interfaces behave like high-resolution telemetry consoles. Crisp 1px structural dividers, mechanical typographic rhythm, and targeted signal accents convey trust, verification, and inspection-grade certainty.
- **Micro-tactility**: Surface physics are firm and tactile rather than floaty. Cards, chips, and controls feel like physical vehicle components—machined, snap-fitted, and clearly demarcated.
- **Uzbek Localization First**: Typography and layout scales accommodate Latin-script Uzbek nomenclature (e.g., *Boqish*, *Ko‘rikdan o‘tgan*, *Xarid qilish*, *Hisobot*) with deliberate horizontal breathing room and calibrated text metrics.

## Colors

The palette establishes an authoritative, high-contrast engineering framework. Surface architecture relies on crisp daylight tones, anchored by stark graphite ink and signaled by electric telemetry blues and algorithmic emerald verifications.

### Hierarchy & Functional Application
- **Graphite Core (`#0F172A`)**: The primary brand anchor. Used for primary interactive actions, high-emphasis headings, key telemetry figures, and primary tactile indicators. It provides industrial weight and immediate grounding.
- **Cobalt Velocity (`#1D4ED8`)**: The secondary actionable accent. Drives active navigation targets, real-time diagnostic indicators, interactive links, highlighted filters, and state toggles.
- **Verification Emerald (`#059669` / `#10B981`)**: The tertiary authority channel. Reserved strictly for trust signals: "Ko‘rikdan o‘tgan" (Inspected), AI VIN verification badges, authentic dealer validation, and positive technical diagnostics.
- **Diagnostic Amber (`#D97706` / `#F59E0B`)**: Alert channel for automotive warnings, service schedules, wear-and-tear thresholds, and urgent vehicle market shifts.
- **Structural Surfaces (`#F8FAFC` base, `#FFFFFF` card layers, `#E2E8F0` border trace)**: Engineered whites and slate-tinted canvas backdrops that isolate vehicle imagery and numerical specifications without glare.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy implements an engineering-led duality: **Space Grotesk** serves as the display and telemetry instrument face, providing automotive cockpit character, while **Geist** delivers zero-compromise precision for vehicle specs, legal verifications, and marketplace body copy.

### Usage Instructions
- **Space Grotesk (Display & Headlines)**: Apply to headline tiers (`display-lg` through `headline-sm`) and technical readout blocks (`telemetry-num`, currency sums in UZS/USD, mileage counters, engine displacements). Maintain negative tracking to retain compact density.
- **Geist (Body & Micro-labels)**: Apply to vehicle condition descriptions, diagnostic logs, and form inputs. Use neutral rendering weights (400 regular, 600 semi-bold) to ensure legibility on low-brightness mobile screens in direct sunlight.
- **Special Characters in Uzbek**: All single quote characters, apostrophes, and inverted commas (`o‘`, `g‘`, `sh`, `ch`) must be rendered with standardized unicode values (`‘` and `’`) ensuring no unexpected line breaks or glyph substitutions occur across mobile devices.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model utilizes a fluid 12-column grid on desktop/tablet views and a single-axis modular column on mobile handheld viewports, strictly calibrated against a 4px/8px industrial metric cadence.

### Grid Architectures
- **Mobile Handheld (360px – 639px)**: 4 fluid columns, `16px` (`1rem`) outer margins, `12px` gutters. Ergonomic focus rests within the lower 40% screen quadrant for thumb-driven filtering, car bidding, and diagnostic activation.
- **Tablet & Dashboard Consoles (640px – 1023px)**: 8 fluid columns, `24px` margins, `16px` gutters. Two-pane split configurations prioritize technical vehicle schematics alongside marketplace metadata.
- **Desktop Command (1024px+)**: 12 columns capped at `1280px` max-width, `32px` gutters, `48px` outer margins. High-density display permits simultaneous display of multi-angle photos, vehicle history checks, and real-time loan simulators.

### Vertical Spacing & Rhythm
- Inter-card spacing within marketplace feeds adheres strictly to `space-md` (`1rem`).
- Diagnostic parameter rows within spec sheets utilize `space-sm` (`0.5rem`) vertical padding to optimize scanning efficiency.
- Major content chapters (e.g., from Car Overview to Inspection Report) separate by `space-xl` (`2.5rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on exaggerated drop shadows. In keeping with automotive and high-grade diagnostics equipment, depth is generated through structural border traces, subtle ambient shielding, and disciplined tonal stacking.

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base (`#F8FAFC`)**: The ground layer. All vehicle scrolling sheets and super-app modules sit on this cool, matte surface.
- **Layer 1 Surface (`#FFFFFF`)**: Primary cards, input containers, and structural tiles. Layer 1 uses a sharp perimeter border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) reinforced by an ambient grounding shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Layer 2 Raised (`#FFFFFF`)**: Floating search toolbars, dynamic filter sheets, and bottom navigation bars. Border remains `1px solid #CBD5E1` with an elevated, diffused contact shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Layer 3 Modals & Vehicle Inspection Drawers**: Overlaid on a tinted slate backdrop (`#0F172A` at 45% opacity). Ambient boundary shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a machined, structured geometry defined by subtle corner radius metrics. In alignment with precision engineering, radii avoid bulbous or overly organic curves.

### Corner Architecture
- **Standard UI Controls (`0.25rem` / `4px`)**: Applied to compact technical pills, data cell badges, status tags, and inline text inputs.
- **Card Containers & Modules (`rounded-lg` / `0.5rem` / `8px`)**: Applied to vehicle summary cards, marketplace listings, spec sheets, and photo carousels.
- **Large Dialogue Modules (`rounded-xl` / `0.75rem` / `12px`)**: Applied exclusively to bottom navigation action sheets, modal dialogs, and AI diagnostic assessment overlays.
- **Border Integration**: Every rounded boundary maintains a continuous 1px outline with interior sub-elements retaining concentric geometric offsets.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Industrial Button**: Solid `#0F172A` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `Space Grotesk` medium font, 4px corner radius. Micro-interaction: quick 80ms scale down (`transform: scale(0.98)`) and fill shift to `#1E293B` on press.
- **Diagnostic Cobalt Action**: Solid `#1D4ED8` background, `#FFFFFF` text. Used for main transactional calls ("Ko‘rish", "Bog‘lanish", "Kredit hisoblash"). Active state deepens to `#1E40AF`.
- **Secondary Outlined**: Transparent fill, 1px border `#CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`. Hover transitions border to `#0F172A`.

### Badges & Verification Chips
- **AI Match / Ko‘rikdan o‘tgan (Verified)**: Subtle emerald tint background (`#ECFDF5`), crisp emerald border (`#A7F3D0`), text `#065F46` with an accompanying checkmark icon.
- **Technical Specs Chip**: Background `#F1F5F9`, border `#E2E8F0`, text `#334155`. Displays displacement, mileage, and fuel type (e.g., `1.5L Turbo • 42,000 km • Benzin`).

### Cards & Vehicle Showcases
- **Listing Unit**: Built on white background with 8px radius and `#E2E8F0` border. Vehicle imagery maintains a fixed 16:10 aspect ratio with an edge-to-edge top clip. Typography displays title in Space Grotesk 18px semi-bold, key metrics in Geist 14px muted slate, and price formatted in Space Grotesk 20px bold in Graphite `#0F172A`.
- **Diagnostic Health Matrix**: Grid card displaying mechanical sub-systems (Dvigatel, Uzatmalar qutisi, Kuzov, Xodovoy) featuring green, amber, or grey status pips.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields**: 44px min-height for thumb clearance. Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#CBD5E1`, internal padding `10px 14px`. On focus: border shifts directly to `#1D4ED8` with a 1px solid halo ring (`rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 18px square with 3px radius for checkboxes; 18px circle for radio buttons. Active state fills with `#0F172A` displaying a centered white glyph.

### Mobile Navigation Bar
- Grounded to the screen bottom with high touch targets (56px bar height). Features an ultra-clean 5-tab configuration: *Bosh sahifa* (Home), *Qidiruv* (Search), *Sotish* (Sell), *Garaj* (My Garage), *Profil* (Profile). Selected item triggers a `#1D4ED8` indicator icon and text treatment.
